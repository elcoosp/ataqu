# Surface brief — Landing (ataqu.com)

FORM: seed:406589551f9e0015 (direction roll, mode: persuade; assigned candidate 4 of 7 grounded list "The Engineering Drawing Set"). Mode: Persuade. Scope: entire Next.js landing (home, pricing, 11 kill sheets, about, blog, faq, roadmap, changelog, legal, 10 app pages).

## THESIS
The homepage is a construction document set from a serious engineering works: Ataqu proves it is built, not marketed. Drafting paper, graphite ink, one redline accent; the architecture section is a cyanotype plate; pricing is a bill of materials; the footer is the title block.

## AUDIENCE / JOB / ACTION
SMB founders, CTOs, ops leads replacing a fragmented stack. Job: verify engineering credibility and pricing math, then join the waitlist. Primary action: the waitlist form, reached from the hero stamp-button and every section's redline link.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
Sheet 01 "GENERAL ARRANGEMENT": display headline set as a drawing title, the 10-app stack drawn in plan with dimension lines that extend on load (the one authored motion moment), title-block strip with sheet metadata, primary CTA as the approval action.

## VISITOR PATH
Hero drawing → bill of materials (pricing table as parts schedule) → parts index (10 apps with callout numbers) → cyanotype architecture plate → escape hatch (release clause) → reference jobs (testimonials) → specification (FAQ) → the form, then the title block closes the sheet.

## SIGNATURE INTERACTION
The hero's dimension lines and stack outline draw themselves once on load (stroke-dashoffset, damped, exponential ease-out, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion). Everything else is quiet: hover states, focus rings, no scroll reveals.

## CROSS-SURFACE REACH
Kill sheets = redline revisions against a competitor's document; pricing = bill of materials; apps = parts schedule entries; changelog = revision table; footer = title block. Blog and legal inherit the same sheet grammar.

## HONEST RISK
Drafting-paper worlds can read as theme-park nostalgia if over-performed; mitigation: hairlines carry structure only where a real plate exists, mono only for numerals/dimensions, accent reserved by palette law for the primary action and live truth.

## UNRESOLVED
None blocking; app icon PNGs are kept as authored parts.
