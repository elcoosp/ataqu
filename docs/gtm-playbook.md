## 1. THE STRATEGIC GTM FOUNDATION

### 1.1 The Core Motion: Product-Led Growth (PLG)

Ataqu is self-serve. There are no "Book a Demo" walls, no mandatory sales calls, and no gated whitepapers. The product is the salesperson.

**2026 Context:** 60% of SaaS companies now identify as product-led, up from 35% in 2021. PLG is no longer a differentiator—it's the default expectation. Users no longer tolerate long onboarding processes, complex documentation, or multi-day setup periods. They expect a product to start delivering value within minutes of first touch.

- **Time-to-Value (TTV):** Top-quartile teams get users to their first activation event in under five minutes. The 2026 standard is being redefined to **under 60 seconds**. Ataqu's Rust backend and 150ms optimistic UI must deliver first-value in under 3 minutes.
- **The UX as Sales:** The speed of the Rust backend, the zero-bloat UI, and the 150ms optimistic interactions are the primary conversion drivers. If the product feels fast, the buyer assumes the underlying architecture is bulletproof.

### 1.2 The Category Positioning: "The Unified SMB OS"

We are not selling a "cheaper CRM" or a "Slack alternative." We are selling the end of the fragmented SaaS stack. Every GTM campaign must frame the problem as *fragmentation* and the solution as *unification*.

**2026 Market Context:** The "SaaSpocalypse" is accelerating. 68% of companies are planning to cut their software stack this year (Gartner). Three of every five SaaS companies lost 30%+ of their customer base in H2 2025 because customers built internal replacements. The middle of the SaaS market isn't consolidating—it's collapsing. Ataqu is perfectly positioned as the rescue mission.

### 1.3 The Ultimate GTM Objective: Decommissioning

Success is not just acquiring a user; success is the user canceling a competitor subscription. We measure GTM efficacy by the "Decommission Rate"—how many competitor tools are actively canceled within 30 days of an Ataqu upgrade.

**The Opportunity:** 35% of companies have already replaced one SaaS tool with a custom build, and 78% expect to build more of their own tools in 2026. This means the market is primed for replacement—they just need a better alternative.

## 2. THE IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (ICP) & TARGETING

We do not target "everyone." We target the specific profiles experiencing acute, active pain.

### 2.1 The Primary ICP: The Capital-Efficient Scale-Up

- **Size:** 10–200 employees.
- **Industry:** B2B SaaS, Digital Agencies, E-commerce, Tech-Enabled Services.
- **Characteristics:** They use HubSpot/Slack/Notion/Zapier. They are profitable (or trying to be) and are scrutinizing their P&L. They have a technical founder or CTO who appreciates good architecture.
- **Spend Profile:** A 10-person business spends ~$35,000/year on SaaS. A 25-person company spends ~$75,000/year. A 50-person company spends ~$142,000/year. The average company uses **106 SaaS applications**, and 36% of those licenses go unused.

### 2.2 Trigger Events (When to strike)

We target companies experiencing "SaaS Shock." A prospect who just experienced a trigger event is **5-10x more likely to respond to cold outreach**.

1.  **Renewal Season:** Detecting when companies are 30-60 days out from their HubSpot or Slack annual renewal. **The Outreach Timing Window:** 0–7 days after the signal delivers peak reply rates of 15–25%.
2.  **Price Hike Announcements:** When a competitor announces a per-user increase (e.g., Slack's 30% hike, HubSpot's 25% jump), we launch targeted intercept campaigns within 24 hours. **Every SaaS is bolting on AI premium** — $40/seat on HubSpot, $50/seat on Notion, $30/seat on Slack. Your SaaS stack just got 20–30% more expensive overnight.
3.  **Funding Rounds (Seed/Series A):** Newly funded startups building their tech stacks. We intercept them before the enterprise sales reps lock them into 3-year contracts.
4.  **New hire in your target role** — they are looking to make changes.

## 3. THE "LAND & EXPAND" WEDGE STRATEGY

We do not force users to buy the $49/mo bundle on day one. We lower the barrier to entry to near-zero, then expand via native value.

### 3.1 The Wedge (Land)

- **The Free Tier:** Unlimited time, strict usage limits. Used for single-users testing PIVOT or SOND.
- **The Starter Plan ($15/mo):** A team adopts one app (e.g., CINQ CRM) for $15/mo because it's cheaper than HubSpot and doesn't require a credit card. They get the full app with no per‑user fees.
- **The Hook:** The UI is fast. The data is clean. The 1-click cancel button gives them psychological safety.

**2026 PLG Insight:** Hybrid go-to-market models that blend PLG with sales-assisted expansion deliver outcomes like **36% free-to-paid conversion** and **3:1 LTV:CAC ratios**.

### 3.2 The Expansion (Expand)

- **The Native Integration Pull:** Once CINQ is adopted, the team realizes they need to notify the team when a deal is won. They see a native banner in CINQ: *"Connect DIAL to get deal-won notifications. 1 click."*
- **The Zapier Eradication:** The user realizes they don't need Zapier anymore. They toggle on SPARK.
- **The Upgrade Path:** The user sees that they are using 3 apps individually. They are prompted: *"You're using 3 apps. Upgrade to Pro ($39/mo) and save $6/mo compared to 3 separate Starters. Or get all 10 with Suite ($79/mo)."* They upgrade, and the competitor stack is officially decommissioned.

Once a user is in the platform, our primary goal is to help them cancel other tools. We turn the product itself into an active decommissioning engine.

### 7.1 The "Replace Your Stack" Dashboard

Inside the Ataqu app, there is a "Stack Decommission" checklist UI.
- **The UI:** It lists common competitors (Slack, HubSpot, Zapier, Notion).
- **The Action:** When a user activates the Ataqu equivalent (e.g., DIAL), they check off "Slack." The UI provides a step-by-step migration guide on how to export Slack data and cancel the Slack account.
- **Gamification:** As they check off tools, a progress bar shows them how much money they are saving per month – based on their current plan (Starter, Pro, or Suite). Upgrading to Pro or Suite unlocks even greater savings.

**2026 Retention Context:** You're not managing adoption anymore—you're managing integration depth and workflow criticality. The Decommission Dashboard positions Ataqu as the hub, not just another tool.

### 7.2 The Native Integration Prompts

When a user creates a form in SOND, the UI immediately prompts: *"Send form responses automatically to CINQ (CRM) and notify the team in DIAL. Activate native integration?"*
This creates an immediate "Aha!" moment where the user realizes the value of the Unified OS over the fragmented stack.

## 9. GTM METRICS & NORTH STARS

We do not measure vanity metrics. We measure capital efficiency and active churn from competitors.

1.  **The Decommission Rate (Primary):** Of the users who upgrade to the $49 bundle, what percentage actively cancel a competitor subscription (verified via migration tool usage) within 30 days?
2.  **Time-to-First-Value (TTFV):** The minutes from signup to the first activation event. Target: <3 minutes.
3.  **Time-to-First-Native-Integration (TTFNI):** How long does it take for a new user to connect two Ataqu apps together? (Measures the success of the Land & Expand wedge).
4.  **PLG Conversion Rate:** Percentage of free-tier users who upgrade to a paid tier without speaking to a human. Target: >36%.
5.  **CAC Payback Period (Community-Led):** Since we do not run expensive enterprise sales cycles, our CAC is primarily engineering and content time. CAC payback must be under 1 month per $49 tenant.
6.  **Engineering Inbound Rate:** Number of inbound signups originating from Hacker News, Reddit, or the Engineering Blog. (Measures the Trojan Horse strategy).
