# 🎯 ATAQU KEYWORD STRATEGY & SEO INTERCEPTION MAP — Version 1.3 (Phase 1)
### The Tactical Blueprint for High-Intent Search Domination

> **Executive Note:** Ataqu does not waste time or capital chasing vanity metrics (e.g., ranking #1 for "what is a CRM?"). That is ZIRP-era marketing bloat. We practice **Interception SEO**. We wait until a competitor inflicts pain (a price hike, an outage, a broken integration), and then we intercept the user when they go to Google to find an escape route. This document maps the exact keywords, search intents, and content assets required to capture users actively looking to decommission their SaaS stack.

---

## 1. THE INTERCEPTION PHILOSOPHY

### 1.1 The "Pain-Trigger" Model
Our SEO strategy is event-driven. When HubSpot announces a 6% price increase, or Slack changes its data retention policy, our content engine spins up. We do not write generic listicles. We write highly specific, mathematically brutal comparison pages and migration guides.

### 1.2 The 3 Pillars of Ataqu Search Intent
1. **The Pricing Pain (Transactional):** The user just got an invoice or a renewal notice. They are searching for cheaper alternatives. *Example: "hubspot pricing increase 2026"*
2. **The Integration Pain (Informational):** The user is tired of paying Zapier to fix broken webhooks. They want native tools. *Example: "native crm and chat integration no zapier"*
3. **The Lock‑in Pain (Actionable):** The user wants to leave but doesn't know how to export their data. *Example: "how to export hubspot contacts to csv"*

## 3. THE "KILL SHEET" SEO ANATOMY

A Kill Sheet is not just a landing page; it is an SEO weapon. Every Kill Sheet must follow this strict technical and structural anatomy to rank.

### 3.1 The H1 & Title Tag
*   **Rule:** Must include the competitor name and the word "Alternative".
*   **Example:** `Ataqu vs. HubSpot: The $49/mo Alternative to 3‑Year Lock‑in`
*   **Meta Description:** `Stop paying $1,200/mo for HubSpot. Ataqu offers a native CRM, Chat, Automation, and Analytics suite for $49/mo flat. 1‑click cancel. Migrate today.`

### 3.2 The TCO (Total Cost of Ownership) Table
*   **SEO Value:** Google loves tables for "pricing" intent. It gets pulled into Featured Snippets.
*   **Content:** A 3‑year cost comparison between the competitor (with add‑ons) and Ataqu ($49/mo). Highlight the architectural superiority: PostgreSQL with unified outbox, LISTEN/NOTIFY, RLS vs. Zapier/webhooks.

### 3.3 The "How to Migrate" Section
*   **SEO Value:** Captures the "how to export" search intent.
*   **Content:** Step‑by‑step instructions with screenshots of the competitor's export menu. This proves we are actually trying to help them leave, not just selling to them.

### 3.4 Internal Linking
*   **Rule:** Every Kill Sheet must link to the specific Ataqu app page (e.g., `/alternatives/hubspot` links to `/apps/cinq`).
*   **Rule:** Every Kill Sheet must link to the Engineering Blog post that proves our architecture is better (e.g., link to `/blog/rust-postgres-architecture`).

## 4. EXECUTION & CADENCE

We do not hire an agency to write 500‑word SEO articles. We build high‑quality, high‑density assets.

### 4.1 The Content Schedule (Per Quarter)
1.  **5 Kill Sheets:** One for each major competitor (HubSpot, Slack, Zapier, Notion, Zoho).
2.  **10 Migration Guides:** Two for each competitor (e.g., "How to export deals", "How to cancel contract").
3.  **4 Engineering Deep‑Dives:** One per month, targeting the CTO persona – focusing on the Phase 1 architecture (PostgreSQL, SeaORM 2.0, raw SQL escape hatch, unified outbox with RLS, advisory locks, JSONB query tiers).
4.  **1 Interactive Tool:** The "SaaS Sprawl Calculator" (a React widget that lets users input their current tools and see their 3‑year savings with Ataqu).

### 4.2 Technical SEO Requirements
*   **Page Speed:** All marketing pages must load in under 1.5s (LCP). No heavy JavaScript. Server‑rendered React (or static HTML) where possible.
*   **Schema Markup:** Use `FAQPage` and `Product` schema on all Kill Sheets to capture rich snippets in Google.
*   **Sitemaps:** Dynamically generated XML sitemaps submitted to Google Search Console.

## 5. MEASURING SEO SUCCESS

We do not measure "organic traffic." We measure "Decommission‑Driven Traffic."

1.  **Assisted Decommission Rate:** Of the users who upgraded to the $49 bundle, what percentage landed on Ataqu via a Kill Sheet or Migration Guide?
2.  **Featured Snippet Capture Rate:** How many of our Target Keywords are we capturing the Google Featured Snippet for? (This is the #1 indicator of Kill Sheet quality).
3.  **Time‑to‑Rank:** How long does it take a new Migration Guide to hit Page 1? (Target: 30 days for low‑difficulty, long‑tail queries).
