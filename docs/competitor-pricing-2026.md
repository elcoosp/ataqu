| Plan | Price | Tasks/Month | Key Limitations |
|------|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Free | $0 | 100 tasks | 1‑step zaps only, limited apps |
| Starter | $29.99/mo | 750 tasks | Multi‑step, no filter/formatting |
| Professional | $49/mo | 2,000 tasks | Filters, formatting, 6‑step workflows |
| Team | $69/mo | 2,000 tasks | Shared workflows + folder management |
| Company | $139/mo | 4,000 tasks | Advanced admin + permissions |

**Annual increase:** No formal announcement, but task limits haven't increased in 5 years.

**Trustpilot:** ⭐ 1.3/5 (310 reviews) — "non‑refundable, support is rude."
