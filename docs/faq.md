# ❓ Ataqu — Frequently Asked Questions

> *Answers to the most common questions from SMB leaders, CTOs, and ops teams.*

---

## 1. Pricing & Billing

**Q1: How much does Ataqu cost?**
**A:** $15/mo for 1 app, $39/mo for 5 apps, or $79/mo for all 10 apps. No per‑user fees, no hidden charges.

**Q2: Can I change plans at any time?**
**A:** Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade in 1 click from your dashboard. Billing is prorated.

**Q3: Is there a long‑term contract?**
**A:** No. All plans are month‑to‑month. You can cancel anytime with no penalty.

**Q4: What happens if I exceed my plan limits?**
**A:** There are no usage limits. The price is fixed regardless of volume.

**Q5: Do you offer discounts for non‑profits or startups?**
**A:** Yes. Contact us at `hello@ataqu.so` with your organization details.

## 2. Migration & Data

**Q6: Can I import data from HubSpot / Slack / Notion?**
**A:** Yes. Every Ataqu app has CSV/JSON import tools to migrate your data in minutes.

**Q7: Can I export my data if I leave Ataqu?**
**A:** Yes. You can export all your data in CSV/JSON in 1 click from your dashboard.

**Q8: Is my data compatible with other tools?**
**A:** Exports are in standard formats (CSV, JSON) for easy reuse.

**Q9: How long does an import take?**
**A:** Imports are usually instant, even for files with thousands of rows.

**Q10: Can you help me migrate?**
**A:** Yes. Our human support is available 24/7 to help you format files and run the migration.
